#VegStore:Privacy Policy

Welcome to VegStore, your trusted online vegetable delivery app. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have regarding your information.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve our services:

1. Personal Information

When you create an account or place an order, we may collect:

Name

Phone number

Delivery address

2. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored only on your device’s local cache or internal storage.

No personal or order information is sent to or stored on any external server.
   
3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to:

Process and deliver your vegetable orders

Communicate with you about your orders or offers

4. Permissions Used

The app may request  permission (like Internet ) only to enable its core features, such as loading images or managing your cart. No background data collection or tracking occurs.

5. Your Control Over Data

You have full control of your data. You can:

Clear the app’s data from device settings

Uninstall the app to remove all stored information

Reinstall the app anytime to start fresh

6. Children’s Privacy

VegStore is intended for general audiences but does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.
