This privacy policy applies to the Android app Babubhai Shakwala (package name com.companyname.vegstore), published on Google Play by the developer Rahul B Chauhan. In the app this product is also referred to as VegStore.

Welcome to Babubhai Shakwala, your trusted online vegetable delivery app. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have regarding your information.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve our services:

1. Personal Information

When you create an account or place an order, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address

2. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored only on your device’s local cache or internal storage.

No personal or order information is sent to or stored on any external server.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to:

  • Process and deliver your vegetable orders
  • Communicate with you about your orders or offers

4. Permissions Used

The app may request certain permissions (like Internet, location, or storage access) only to enable its core features, such as loading images or managing your cart. No background data collection or tracking occurs.

5. Your Control Over Data

You have full control of your data. You can:

  • Clear the app’s data from device settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all stored information
  • Reinstall the app anytime to start fresh

6. Children’s Privacy

Babubhai Shakwala is intended for general audiences but does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

7. The list of permissions

Need internet access to view the live prices of the vegetables.

Need location access to confirm the delivery is inside Bhavnagar and to fill the current address. Location is used only when you open the app or place an order.

  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
  • android.permission.INTERNET
  • android.permission.ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
  • android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
